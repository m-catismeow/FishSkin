# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, for Minecraft 1.20.1 (branch `main`) and 1.21.1 / 1.21.4 (branch `1.21.x`), Fabric and Forge.

- HD skins up to 2048 px, a wardrobe of outfits, wide or slim arms.
- The open key works anywhere by default; the Skin Station and listed blocks, and skin zones, can open the
  wardrobe too (off by default).
- `config/fishskin.toml` (TOML, with `#` comments); the server's settings are used in multiplayer.
- Figura avatars per outfit, with a searchable picker that shows each avatar's colour (when Figura is installed).
- Plasmo Voice talking skins that follow your actual voice level (when Plasmo Voice is installed).
- Skins stay in memory only, on the server and on clients.
